# Dynamic Filtering System - Practical Examples

## Real-World Filtering Scenarios

This document provides concrete examples of how customers and staff can use the filtering system to find the perfect products.

---

## 📋 Customer Use Cases

### Use Case 1: "I'm Planning a Wedding and Want Roses"

**Customer Goal**: Find elegant rose arrangements suitable for a wedding

**How to Filter**:
1. Click **Weddings** category button
2. Click **Roses** flower type button
3. Browse the filtered results

**Expected Results**:
- Products: JULIETTE, KYSANDRA, MARZELLINE, CHAI (4 products)
- All are wedding-appropriate rose arrangements
- Mixed price range: ₱2,390 - ₱3,500

**Why This Works**:
- Combines two relevant criteria using AND logic
- Shows only what matches BOTH conditions
- Saves time vs. browsing all 49 products

## 👔 Staff-Assisted Use Cases

### Scenario 1: Helping a Confused Customer

**Customer**: "I don't know where to start, what do you recommend?"

**Staff Action**:
1. Ask: "What's the occasion?" (Birthday, Wedding, Event, etc.)
2. Click appropriate category button
3. Ask: "Any favorite flowers?" (Roses, Tulips, etc.)
4. Click flower type button
5. Show refined results

**Result**: Personalized recommendations in seconds

---

### Scenario 2: Handling "I'm Not Sure What I Want"

**Customer**: "Show me something nice in my budget of ₱1,500"

**Staff Action**:
1. Don't filter by category/flower type yet
2. Browse and manually check price badges
3. Click View Details on products near ₱1,500
4. Show full pricing for different sizes
5. Help customer decide

**Result**: Browsing feels targeted despite loose criteria

---

### Scenario 3: Event Planners Ordering in Bulk

**Planner**: "We need 20 fresh rose arrangements for a wedding"

**Staff Action**:
1. Click **Fresh** category
2. Click **Roses** flower type
3. Show all fresh roses
4. Click View Details on arrangements with size/quantity options
5. Help calculate total for 20 units

**Result**: Easy identification of suitable products for bulk order

---

### Scenario 4: Seasonal Recommendations

**Staff**: "Let me show you what's fresh this week"

**Action**:
1. Click **Fresh** category
2. Browse seasonal availability
3. Recommend based on what's available

**Result**: Customers see fresh, in-season options first

---

### Scenario 5: Similar Product Suggestions

**Customer**: "I like this product but want to see something similar"

**Staff Action**:
1. Note the flower type (e.g., Roses)
2. Click **Roses** filter
3. Show other rose options at similar or different price points
4. Highlight differences and similarities

**Result**: Customer can easily compare similar products

## 🎓 Teaching Customers

### Simple Explanation

> "We have two ways to find products. First, pick what you're looking for (Fresh, Dried, Wedding). Then, pick your favorite flower (Roses, Tulips, Lilies, etc.). The system shows only products matching both choices!"

### Visual Walkthrough

1. Show category buttons: "Pick the occasion"
2. Show flower type buttons: "Pick the flower"
3. Show results: "Here's what matches both!"
4. Show Clear Filters: "Start over anytime"

## 📈 Conversion Optimization

### Best Practices for Higher Sales

1. **Start with Most Popular**: Fresh category by default
2. **Visual Guidance**: Highlight Roses (most products)
3. **Price Transparency**: Show price badges clearly
4. **Quick Alternatives**: "Similar products" via filters
5. **Reduce Choice Paralysis**: Use filters to narrow options
